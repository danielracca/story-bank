# Harvest Prompt Library

Phase 1 prompts. Fire in batches of three or four. Ask for one-line answers only.

Good prompts point at a *moment*, not a *quality*. "Tell me about a time you showed leadership" gets an abstraction. "When did you have to tell someone something they didn't want to hear?" gets a scene.

---

## Universal set — use with everyone

- What is something you built or shipped that you are still proud of?
- When did you change someone's mind about something that mattered?
- What is the biggest thing you got wrong?
- When did you have to say no to someone more senior than you?
- What is the hardest conversation you have had at work?
- When did you find out you had been wrong about something for a long time?
- What is a moment you thought it was going to fall apart?
- What did you do that nobody asked you to do?
- When did you have to learn something fast, from nothing?
- Who taught you something you still use? What happened?
- What is the closest call you have had?
- When did you have to clean up someone else's mess?
- What is a decision you made with not enough information?
- What is a project everyone remembers for the wrong reason?
- What is something that worked for a reason nobody expected?

## Vulnerability set — always run this one

Most people arrive with only wins. These stories are what make them trusted.

- When did you feel out of your depth?
- What did you fail at that you still think about?
- When did you have to admit something publicly?
- Who did you let down?
- What did you avoid for too long?
- When did you get feedback that stung and turned out to be right?
- What is something you used to be sure about and are not anymore?
- When were you the least confident person in the room?

## Humour set — short, light, 30 seconds max

- What is the funniest thing that has gone wrong on the job?
- What is a running joke on your team, and where did it come from?
- What is the most absurd meeting you have sat in?
- What did you completely misunderstand at the start of your career?
- What is something embarrassing that turned out fine?
- What is the strangest request anyone has made of you?

---

## Job interviews

- Why did you leave each place? What actually happened?
- What is the thing on your CV people always ask about?
- What is a piece of work that only you could have done?
- When did you influence something above your level?
- When did you disagree with a leader and what did you do about it?
- What did you have to cut, and how did you decide?
- What is your best evidence that you operate at the level you are interviewing for?
- What is the gap in your background someone will probe? What is the honest story?

## Client & sales calls

- Which customer changed how you see the product?
- When did you talk someone out of buying something?
- What is a deal you lost, and what did it teach you?
- When did a customer use it in a way you never intended?
- What is the objection you hear most, and when did you answer it best?
- Who was sceptical and came around? What flipped them?
- When did you deliver something that beat the promise?

## Leading a team

- When did you have to give hard feedback?
- Who did you hire that changed the team?
- When did you protect the team from something?
- When did you make a call the team disagreed with?
- What did you get wrong as a manager?
- When did you have to hold a line under pressure?
- Who grew on your watch, and what did you actually do?

## Networking & rapport

- What is the story you always end up telling at dinner?
- How did you end up doing this work at all?
- What is a coincidence that changed your path?
- What is the most unusual place your work has taken you?
- What do you do outside work that surprises people?
- What is a small thing you are irrationally good at?

## Talks & presentations

- What is the belief you would defend in front of a hostile room?
- What does your industry get wrong that you can prove?
- What is the moment that turned an opinion of yours into a conviction?
- What would you tell yourself five years ago?
- What is the example you reach for when explaining what you do to a stranger?

---

## Chasing a half-mentioned story

When someone drops something and moves past it — "that was around the time everything blew up, but anyway" — stop and go back:

- "Wait. Go back to the part where everything blew up."
- "You said that like it was nothing. What happened?"
- "You skipped something there."

These recoveries produce the best stories in most sessions. People undervalue what they have lived through.
